Part 1 — Create your free Netlify account & deploy the site
1
Go to Netlify and create a free account
Open your browser and go to netlify.com. Click "Sign up" — use your email address or sign up with Google. The free plan is completely sufficient for your website.
2
Deploy your site — drag and drop (easiest method)
Once logged in, you will see the Netlify dashboard. Scroll down until you see a box that says "Want to deploy a new site without connecting to Git?" with a drag-and-drop area underneath it.

Select all 4 files (index.html, netlify.toml, robots.txt, sitemap.xml) from the folder on your computer, then drag them all at once into that box.
Important: Drag all 4 files together — not the folder, the files themselves. On Windows: open the folder, select all (Ctrl+A), drag. On Mac: open the folder, select all (Cmd+A), drag.
3
Your site is live! (with a temporary Netlify URL)
After uploading, Netlify will give you a random URL like https://fancy-alpaca-123456.netlify.app. Your site is already live and working at this address! Open it in your browser to check everything looks correct before connecting your domain.
Part 2 — Connect your domain natural-healthy-village.com
4
Add your custom domain in Netlify
In your Netlify dashboard, click on your new site, then go to Site settings → Domain management → Add a domain. Type in: natural-healthy-village.com and click Verify. Also add www.natural-healthy-village.com.
5
Find your Netlify DNS nameservers
Netlify will show you 4 nameservers that look like this:
dns1.p01.nsone.net
dns2.p01.nsone.net
dns3.p01.nsone.net
dns4.p01.nsone.net
Copy these 4 nameservers — you will need them in the next step.
6
Update nameservers at your domain registrar
Log in to wherever you registered your domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Name.com, etc.). Find the DNS settings or Nameservers section for natural-healthy-village.com. Replace the existing nameservers with the 4 Netlify nameservers you copied in Step 5.
Note: DNS changes take 15 minutes to 48 hours to propagate worldwide. During this time the site may show the old page. This is normal — just wait.
7
Enable free HTTPS (SSL certificate)
Once your domain is connected, go to Site settings → Domain management → HTTPS. Click "Verify DNS configuration" then "Provision certificate". Netlify will automatically install a free SSL certificate. This makes your site https:// which Google requires for good SEO ranking.
This is completely free and automatic — Netlify handles renewals for you forever.
Part 3 — Tell Google your site exists (critical for SEO)
8
Submit to Google Search Console
Go to search.google.com/search-console. Sign in with your Google account. Click "Add property" and enter https://www.natural-healthy-village.com. Google will ask you to verify ownership — choose the HTML tag method and paste the code they give you into the <head> section of your index.html (I can do this for you).
9
Submit your sitemap to Google
In Google Search Console, go to Sitemaps in the left menu. Enter: sitemap.xml and click Submit. Google will now crawl and index your site within a few days.
After this, your site will start appearing in Google search results for terms like "land for sale Kaengkrachan", "ที่ดินขาย แก่งกระจาน", "weekend home near Bangkok" etc.
Part 4 — How to update the site when a plot sells
10
Updating is simple — just re-upload
When a plot sells, tell me and I will send you a new index.html with that plot removed and the count updated (e.g. "5 plots remaining"). Then:

1. Go to your Netlify dashboard
2. Click on your site
3. Go to Deploys
4. Drag the new index.html file into the deploy box
5. Done — the site updates in under 60 seconds worldwide
No technical knowledge needed for updates. The whole process takes about 2 minutes.
