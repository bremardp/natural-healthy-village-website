TOPIC:  Earth-Block-Construction

1. MAIN QUESTION
	- Why build a chapel here?
	- How was build the chapel?
	- What material were used for the construction?
	- Who can use the chapel?
			
2. DIRECT ANSWER
	- The idea of chapel came from some of the project founders which practice meditation and would like to offer to all project participants and visitor a place where they can pursue their spiritual path. The gothic design and use of stone came from a similar chapel in Planes in Pyrennee France which has combine simplicity and quietness. Transmission of our ancestor from middle age. Equally we would like to transmit to future generation a landmark where they can experience humility, contemplation and harmony.  
	- The design was made by the project founder. The construction by a French Stonemasson "Compagnon de tour de France" who train five Thai workers to stone cutting and carving. Surprisingly it took only only three months to train the Thai workers to the level of Stone Masson builder. Usually it take few years to train French worker to this level. It took only three years to build the chapel with a small team of five people. 
	- We made a point to use local material as much as possible. The stone came from a quarray in Saraburi. The stone is four times harder than the stone used to build cathedrals in France, it will last longer. The limestone from Thailand. The roof tile from Ayudaya. The stained glass from Bangkok. The glass material came from France from the last place which produce since middle age the material for the stained glass.
	- The chapel dedication is to Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi incarnation of the adi shakti. Everyone can use the chapel, there is no religion restriction. 
	
	
3. KEY FACTS
     	All stone supports were wood build. Assembly of stone is done without any cement only limestone to allow breathing and movement.
	All stones were properly cut by the Stone Masson and no adjustment was necessary when lined up.
	There are several head carved on the key stone of the arcature. 	
	
	
4. WHAT MAKES THIS UNIQUE TO NHVK
	There is only one chapel in Thailand build with traditional workcraft and material. Also there is only one chapel in the world dedicated to N.D. Shri Mataji.
	The size and location on an island in the project lake make it unforgettable and part of the landscape as it was there for a long time.
