TOPIC:  Land Princing & Plots

1. MAIN QUESTION
	- What is the lowest priced plot?
	- Are prices negotiable?
	- What are the plot size?
		
2. DIRECT ANSWER
	- Plot 2a is the entry plot at 2,500,000 THB for 1,070 m² (2 ngan 70 wah).
	- Contact the project team for current availability and to arrange a site visit. Phone 081 860 4501 (English/French) or 095 365 2500 (Thai).
	- The plot were designed to cover every need between 1,000 up to 2,800 m2. This is sufficient to build a large house, pool, tropical garden and grow your own vegetable and fruit.
	
3. WHAT AFFECTS PRICE
    	Plot price reflects size (1,070–2,824 m²), position, and views. All plots include access to more than 12 rai of common green space, 
	the private lake, walkways, and shared infrastructure. Residential use only — no commercial development.
	All plots have underground electrical power supply 3 phases and 3 line of water supply.

4. WHAT MAKES THIS UNIQUE TO NHVK
	The project was design to provide self sustainability, low density and living comfort with no compromise unless other traditional development
	in which the developper give you only 800 m2 small piece of land and 5% of common area which is what the law require.
	
5. TITLE AND TENURE
	All plots hold Chanote title deeds (the highest land security in Thailand). Thai nationals may purchase freehold.
	Foreign nationals may register a 30-year renewable lease.
