Can foreigners buy land at Natural Healthy Village?

ชาวต่างชาติซื้อที่ดิน — สัญญาเช่า 30 ปี

Foreign nationals may hold a registered 30-year renewable lease on Chanote-backed plots — a secure long-term tenure for residential land in Thailand.

Freehold vs lease

Thai law restricts foreign freehold land ownership. At Natural Healthy Village, Thai buyers may purchase with full Chanote ownership. Foreign buyers typically use a 30-year registered lease, which is a legally recognised long-term tenure commonly used for residential land in Thailand.

Why Chanote matters

Every plot at NHVK holds a Chanote title deed — the most secure form of land title in Thailand. A registered lease on Chanote land provides clarity for long-term residential use and inheritance planning. Always consult a Thai property lawyer for your personal situation.

Frequently asked questions

Can the 30-year lease be renewed?
Leases are commonly structured with renewal options; specific terms are set in the sale/lease agreement and should be reviewed with legal counsel.
Is this a resort or hotel project?
No. Natural Healthy Village is residential land only — a private community for weekend homes or permanent residence, not commercial tourism.
