How much does land cost at Natural Healthy Village?

ราคาที่ดินและแปลงที่มีจำหน่าย

Six residential plots remain from sixteen total, priced from 2,500,000 to 7,000,000 THB with Chanote title deeds.

Plots currently available

As of 2026, six plots remain from sixteen total: Plot 1 (1,396 m², 3,500,000 THB), Plot 2a (1,070 m², 2,500,000 THB), Plot 3 (2,056 m², 5,000,000 THB), Plot 6 (2,824 m², 7,000,000 THB), Plot 9 (2,324 m², 6,500,000 THB), Plot 16 (2,164 m², 5,400,000 THB).

What affects price

Plot price reflects size (1,070–2,824 m²), position, and views. All plots include access to more than 12 rai of common green space, the private lake, walkways, and shared infrastructure. Residential use only — no commercial development.

Title and tenure

All plots hold Chanote title deeds (the highest land security in Thailand). Thai nationals may purchase freehold. Foreign nationals may register a 30-year renewable lease.
