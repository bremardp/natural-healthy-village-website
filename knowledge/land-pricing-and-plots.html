Plot sizes — room to build and grow

Plots were designed to cover every need between 1,000 and 2,800 m² — enough for a large house, swimming pool, tropical garden, and space to grow your own vegetables and fruit.

What affects price

Plot price reflects size (1,070–2,824 m²), position, and views. All plots include access to more than 12 rai of common green space, the private lake, walkways, and shared infrastructure. Residential use only — no commercial development.

Every plot has underground 3-phase electrical supply with an individual meter and three water supply lines with individual meters. See our solar, water and utilities article for full infrastructure details.

What makes NHVK different

Natural Healthy Village was designed for self-sustainability, low density, and living comfort without compromise. Many traditional developments offer only ~800 m² per plot and the minimum common area required by law (~5%). At NHVK, every owner shares access to more than 12 rai of lakes, walkways, and green space — a fundamentally different proposition.

Title and tenure

All plots hold Chanote title deeds (the highest land security in Thailand). Thai nationals may purchase freehold. Foreign nationals may register a 30-year renewable lease.
