About the Project

A Rare Place to
Call Home

โครงการที่ดินพักอาศัยส่วนตัว แก่งกระจาน

Nestled between the mountains and Kaengkrachan National Park in Phetchaburi Province, Natural Healthy Village is an exclusive residential land community unlike anything else in the region. Designed for those who seek a peaceful weekend retreat, a permanent home in nature, or a self-sustainable lifestyle away from the city.

At the heart of the project lies a private lake with two islands — one home to a stunning chapel, the other to a traditional sala — connected by pedestrian walkways and surrounded by lush green gardens. The mountain backdrop is visible from every plot.

Bangkok is just ~2.5 hours away by road, and the beaches of Hua Hin and Cha-am are only 40 km. This is the Kaengkrachan equivalent of what Khao Yai became for Bangkok weekenders — a natural sanctuary within easy reach of the city.

Residential use only — no resorts, no commercial development. A true private community.
